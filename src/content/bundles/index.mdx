## What a bundle holds

- **Every feature for one use case.** The DOCX bundle, for example, brings pages, footnotes, cross-references, comments, tracked changes, and DOCX import and export.
- **One license.** It unlocks all of them at once, without separate plans or add-ons.
- **One install.** You install one package, and its features are tested together.
