## What the buttons do

- The zoom buttons zoom in and out, and fit all shapes into view.
- The color buttons color the selected shapes and the shapes you add next. The demo adds `pink` to the board's colors.
- Export saves the board to a JSON file, and Import opens one, in place of the board or added to it.
- PNG and SVG save the selected shapes, or the whole board, as an image, and Copy copies them as a PNG.
