## When you use them

- **Footnotes.** Footnotes and endnotes that number themselves, in any editor.
- **Cross-references.** References to headings, clauses, or bookmarks that stay up to date.
- **Bookmark.** Named places in the document that links and references can point to.
- **Outline numbering.** Numbers headings and clauses as 1.1, Article I, or (a), from where they stand.
- **Number formats.** One set of number formats for page numbers, notes, and outline numbers.
- **Document styles.** Named styles for paragraphs, headings, and tables, stored in the document. Change a style and everything using it follows.
- **Columns.** Containers side by side, each with content of its own.

## How they work

- **They come with Page Layout.** Page Layout lays out a document as pages, and these extensions ship with it.
- **They work without pages.** You can use them in an editor that doesn't lay out pages.
- **Their docs follow.** The cards above show them as coming soon until their pages are published.
