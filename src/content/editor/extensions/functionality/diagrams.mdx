The Diagrams extension adds charts and flow diagrams to a Tiptap editor. A chart keeps its data
and its settings in the document, a diagram its Mermaid, draw.io or BPMN source, and a library of
your choice draws the picture.

<Callout title="More details" variant="hint">
  For detailed information on installation, configuration and every Command, visit the [Diagrams
  feature page](/diagrams/getting-started/overview).
</Callout>

<Callout title="Alpha" variant="info">
  This extension is under active development. The API may change in future releases.
</Callout>
