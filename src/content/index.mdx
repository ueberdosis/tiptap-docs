## Tiptap Platform

Tiptap covers the whole life of a document: the editor, the features your workflow needs, and the [Document Server](/collaboration/getting-started/overview) that stores and syncs documents. [Tiptap Platform](/platform) shows the parts, what each plan includes, and where it runs; [Tiptap Document](/document-model) explains how they share one document.

## What you can build

- **Edit together in real time.** Several people in one document, with cursors and offline support. [Documents](/collaboration/getting-started/overview)
- **Review changes.** Comment threads, suggestions to accept or reject, and comparisons between versions. [Comments](/comments/getting-started/overview), [Tracked Changes](/tracked-changes/getting-started/overview), [Compare](/compare/getting-started/overview)
- **Work with AI.** Agents that read and edit the document, or AI commands in the editor. [AI Toolkit](/ai/ai-toolkit/overview), [Basic AI Generation](/ai/basic/overview)
- **Write documents in pages.** Page formats, headers, footers, and footnotes. [Pages](/pages/getting-started/overview)
- **Convert documents.** Import and export Word, PDF, ODT, EPUB, and Markdown. [Conversion](/conversion/getting-started/overview)
- **Build templates.** Fields to fill, values from your data, and parts only some may edit. [Composable Documents](/composable-docs/slots/getting-started/overview)

[All features](/editor/extensions/overview) lists every feature with its plan, filterable by industry.
