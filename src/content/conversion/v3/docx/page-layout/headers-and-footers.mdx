Each section keeps its headers and footers in its `sectionBreak` node, beside the other page settings that [Sections and page setup](/conversion/v3/docx/page-layout/sections) lists.

## Import headers and footers

Import puts each section's headers and footers into its `sectionBreak`, as `pageFurniture` nodes with a `zone` of `header` or `footer` and one of these `slot` values:

| `slot`    | Shows on                                                       |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `default` | Every page, or the odd pages when odd and even pages differ    |
| `first`   | The first page of the section, when `differentFirstPage` is on |
| `even`    | The even pages, when `differentOddEven` is on                  |

The `sectionBreak` also carries `differentFirstPage`, `differentOddEven`, and the distance of the header and footer from the page edge as `headerOffset` and `footerOffset`, in pixels. Import gives each `sectionBreak` an `id`: `sec-1` for the first section, `sec-2` for the second, and so on.

A Word section set to "Link to Previous" gets no header or footer of its own, and shows the one of the section before it. When several sections share a header or footer in the file, each gets its own copy.

Word's page number fields, `PAGE`, `NUMPAGES` and `SECTIONPAGES`, become `pageNumber` nodes in headers, footers and the body, so they keep counting. Other fields, such as dates, import as the text Word last showed and no longer update.

## Export headers and footers

Export reads the headers and footers from the `pageFurniture` nodes of each section's `sectionBreak`. They hold rich content, such as tables and images, and convert like the body.

Their content comes from the document, so no export option sets it. To format them differently from the body, use `headerFooterOverrides`, as [Header and footer formatting](/conversion/v3/docx/export-styles#header-and-footer-formatting) shows.

- A section can have a `default`, `first` and `even` version of its header and of its footer.
- A section without its own version uses the previous section's.
- A section can use another section's version instead. One of its link attributes then holds the `id` of that section's `sectionBreak`, such as `sec-1`: `headerDefaultLink`, `headerFirstLink`, `headerEvenLink`, `footerDefaultLink`, `footerFirstLink` or `footerEvenLink`.
- `differentFirstPage` exports for each section on its own.
- Word has one odd and even switch for the whole file. Export turns it on when any section has `differentOddEven`. A section without it then gets its `default` version on the even pages too.

The distance of the header and footer from the page edge comes from the section's `headerOffset` and `footerOffset`, 1.25cm by default. One header or footer can set its own distance in the `offset` attribute of its `pageFurniture` node.

Word keeps one distance per section, so when the versions a section shows have different distances, export uses the section's, with an `unsupported` warning.

A `pageNumber` node exports as a live Word field, which its `kind` picks. [Page numbers](/features/page-layout/page-numbers#a-page-number-in-a-header-or-footer) shows how to add one:

| `kind`         | Word field                         |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| `page`         | The page number                    |
| `pages`        | The number of pages                |
| `sectionPages` | The number of pages in the section |
