A whiteboard in React, with buttons for the tools, undo and redo, the zoom and fitting all shapes into view. The color buttons color the selected shapes and the shapes you add next. The demo adds `pink` to the board's colors. The board starts with sticky notes, shapes, arrows and a pen line. Press the key of a tool, like <kbd>N</kbd> for a note, to choose it.
