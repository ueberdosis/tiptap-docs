The Whiteboard extension turns a Tiptap editor into a whiteboard. People add sticky notes, shapes, text, arrows, pen drawings and doc frames to a board, and pan and zoom around it.

<CodeDemo isPro path="/Extensions/Whiteboard" />

The extension is headless. It draws the board and its shapes, but ships no toolbar. Your app adds the controls and the stage around the board.

<Callout title="Alpha" variant="warning">
  Whiteboard is in alpha. Its API may change before a stable release.
</Callout>

## Features

- **Shapes**: sticky notes, rectangles, ellipses, diamonds, triangles and free text
- **Rich text in shapes**: bold, italic, strike, code, links, text colors, highlights, bullet lists, numbered lists and task lists
- **Arrows**: connect two shapes and follow them when they move, or end at a free point
- **Pen and eraser**: draw freehand lines and erase them
- **Doc frames**: show a whole rich text document on the board, in an editor of its own
- **Selection**: select, move, resize, duplicate, copy and paste shapes, and bring them to the front or send them to the back
- **Camera**: pan and zoom with a mouse, a trackpad or the keyboard
- **Undo and redo**: each step selects the shapes that it changed
- **Collaboration**: edit a board together through Yjs, and see what others select and drag
- **Tracked Changes**: show suggestions on shapes, and accept or reject them

## How it works

### The board is one Tiptap document

`WhiteboardKit` brings its own top node. The document holds one `board` node, and the board holds the shapes. So a board loads, saves and syncs like any other Tiptap document, for example as JSON or through Yjs.

### Shapes are nodes

Each shape is a node on the board. Its position and size are attributes, in board units. Sticky notes, geometric shapes and free text hold paragraphs and lists, and people edit their text like any other content. Arrows, pen lines and doc frames are atom nodes.

Every shape has an `id`, and the commands find shapes by their ids. The board keeps the selected shapes apart from the text selection, also by id. While no text is edited, the board has the keyboard: keys choose tools, and move, delete or restack the selected shapes.

### A camera pans and zooms

The stage is the element around the board. Inside it, a viewport element holds the editor. The camera, `{ x, y, zoom }`, is state of your app. `attachStage` turns presses, wheel turns and shortcuts in the stage into camera changes, and your app moves the viewport with `toCssTransform(camera)`. See [Stage and camera](/whiteboard/guides/stage-and-camera).

### Doc frames hold documents of their own

A doc frame is a shape that shows a rich text document in an editor of its own. The board stores the frame and the id of its document. Your app keeps the documents and creates their editors. See [Doc frames](/whiteboard/guides/doc-frames).
