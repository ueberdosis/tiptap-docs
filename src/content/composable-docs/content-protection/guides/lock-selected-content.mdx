To let users select arbitrary content and lock it:

1. Define a [custom Mark or Node](/editor/extensions/custom-extensions/create-new) in your schema. Add an editor command that applies it to the selection.
2. Define a [protection rule](/composable-docs/content-protection/api-reference/policy) that selects content with that Mark or Node and sets the permission to `edit: false` so that its content cannot be edited. Use `target: 'mark'` to protect the marked content and its Mark, or `target: 'node'` to protect the Node, its attributes and all its content.
