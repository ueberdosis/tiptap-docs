Compared to other AI extensions, the AI Toolkit gives you more flexibility and control. Migrating to the AI Toolkit is recommended as you scale your application, to allow for more bespoke AI functionality.

We offer migration guides for the following extensions:

- [Basic AI Generation](/ai/ai-toolkit/client/advanced-guides/migration-guides/ai-generation)
- [AI Suggestion](/ai/ai-toolkit/client/advanced-guides/migration-guides/ai-suggestion)
- [AI Changes](/ai/ai-toolkit/client/advanced-guides/migration-guides/ai-changes)
- [AI Assistant](/ai/ai-toolkit/client/advanced-guides/migration-guides/ai-assistant)

Each guide will help you migrate your existing AI functionality to the AI Toolkit.
